# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Office-going executives (bankers, founders, corporate professionals) who live or work in Gulshan and Banani, Dhaka. They already own a car, usually an automatic sedan or SUV, but depend on a hired driver because of Dhaka traffic anxiety or lack of practice. Most arrive on a phone from LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook links. Their job: decide whether to trust a stranger to teach them in their own car, then book.

## Product Purpose
A solo mentor teaches private, 1-on-1 driving lessons at the client's doorstep, in the client's own car, during the early-morning window before Dhaka traffic builds and before the client's driver arrives. Success is a WhatsApp conversation that turns into a booked diagnostic drive or package.

## Positioning
Lessons happen in your own car, from your own gate, on your own streets, at a time that never touches office hours. Driving schools use shared training cars at distant centers in rushed sessions; this is the opposite on every axis.

## Operating Context
- Sessions run 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM. Beginners start in quiet residential lanes, then Gulshan Avenue and Kemal Ataturk Avenue, then basement and mall parking, flyovers and the real office commute.
- Booking happens on WhatsApp (wa.me/8801922603331) with a pre-filled message. There is no backend.
- A one-page signed safety and liability agreement precedes Session 1. Payment by cash, bKash or bank transfer, upfront or 50/50.
- Reschedule with 10 hours' notice. Packages are valid for 45 days.

## Evidence on Hand
- True and may be stated: BRTA Professional Driving Licence and NID shown to clients; 4+ car owners mentored; every session recorded on a road camera.
- NOT true; must not appear: any monthly slot limit or scarcity claim ("only 4 slots").
- Unverified; keep out of headlines: "BRTA e-Learner Permit in 10 minutes".
- Coming from the owner: real photos (the mentor, a client car, Gulshan/Banani streets at dawn). Until they arrive, use marked slots; never stock or AI-generated people.
- No testimonials, quotes or client names exist. Never invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Trust before persuasion: a stranger will sit beside them in their own car at 6 AM, so verifiable facts lead.
2. Their car, their street, their schedule: every section should feel local to Gulshan and Banani, not generic driving instruction.
3. One clear next step: a WhatsApp conversation, never a maze of buttons.
4. Say only what is true today.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Phone-first in in-app browsers; thumb-reachable primary action, 44px tap targets, readable contrast in bright morning light, reduced motion respected.
